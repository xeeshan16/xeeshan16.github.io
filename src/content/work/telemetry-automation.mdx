## Context

Operational signals were spread across systems, and engineers assembled the picture by hand for every incident.

## Approach

- FastAPI services that ingest and analyse operational telemetry.
- Kafka consumers that react to known event patterns and trigger automated steps, so routine work doesn't wait for a person.
- OpenTelemetry Collector pipelines on Azure AKS and in the bank's on-premises bare-metal and air-gapped environments, so telemetry looks the same wherever a service runs.

## Key decisions

**One pipeline, every environment.** The same OpenTelemetry Collector setup runs on AKS and in the bank's on-premises bare-metal and air-gapped environments. In air-gapped zones, a local Collector sends to a gateway Collector, which exports to the bank's internal observability backend, so nothing needs a route to the internet.

**Correlate before you alert.** Metrics, logs and traces carry the same trace IDs and service and environment attributes, so an alert links straight to the traces and logs behind it instead of starting a search.

## Production story

### Rebuilding the same picture by hand on every incident

**What happened.** Operational signals were spread across systems. Every incident started the same way: the on-call engineer pulled signals together by hand before they could start fixing anything, and routine issues with well-known causes still waited for a person to notice and act.

**The fix.** I split the work into two flows on the same Kafka stream:

- **Known patterns are automated.** Kafka consumers watch for event patterns with known responses and trigger the automated step directly, so routine work doesn't wait for a person.
- **Everything else arrives pre-assembled.** FastAPI services ingest and analyse the telemetry, so when an engineer is needed they start from an analysed picture instead of a blank page.
- **Signals are connected, not just collected.** Because metrics, logs and traces share trace IDs and service and environment attributes, the picture an engineer receives already links the alert to the traces and logs behind it.

## Outcome

The services handle more than 100,000 operational events a day and cut incident troubleshooting time by about 40%. Correlated metrics, logs and traces shortened both the time to detect an incident (MTTD) and the time to resolve it (MTTR).
